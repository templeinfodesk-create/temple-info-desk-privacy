Privacy Policy – Temple Info Desk

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Temple Info Desk is an independent browser extension that helps users fill supported TTD booking forms.

Information We Handle

Users may save information such as name, age, gender, photo ID type and number, email, city, state, country, pincode, and Gothram.

How Information Is Used

Saved information is used only to provide the extension's form-filling functionality and reduce repetitive manual data entry.

Storage

User-provided information is stored locally in the user's Chrome browser using Chrome extension storage. We do not sell or transfer this information to third parties.

Login and OTP

The extension does not handle TTD login credentials, passwords, OTPs, or other authentication credentials. Users complete login and OTP verification themselves.

Queue and Security

The extension does not bypass, skip, or circumvent TTD virtual queues, waiting rooms, CAPTCHA, security mechanisms, availability restrictions, or booking limits.

Payments

The extension does not process or collect payment information. Users complete payments through the applicable official booking website.

Booking

The extension assists with supported form filling. Users are responsible for reviewing their information and completing final booking actions. The extension does not guarantee ticket availability or successful booking.

Data Sharing

User information is not sold, rented, or transferred to third parties and is used only for the extension's stated purpose.

Deleting Data

Users can edit or delete saved information through the extension or by removing the extension and its local data from Chrome.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.

Independent Extension

Temple Info Desk is an independent extension. It is not an official TTD application or service and is not endorsed or sponsored by TTD.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the support contact provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
